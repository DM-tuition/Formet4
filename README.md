# formet — version 4

The fourth design study for **formet**, the Manchester knitwear label, built on the owner's own
marks: his wordmark, damask and woven tag (traced from his files), every product shot cut out on
white, and his palette of burgundy on white and a very light cream.

**Live:** https://dm-tuition.github.io/Formet4/

- A short intro: the damask, then the wordmark, then the page.
- The three pieces stand in front of a giant wordmark that shrinks into the header as you scroll.
- A Mertra-style grid with quick add, Zara-style full-screen product pages, a sideways lookbook.
- The No. 1 dive through the back print (with the glass lens) and the Manchester banner, both on a
  smoothed scrub so fast scrolling stays smooth.

This is not the official store. The bag and checkout are a demo: nothing is sold and no card
details are asked for. Photos and marks are the brand's own.
